# Gym plan: strength, size, tendons, skills (12 weeks)

Status: BUILT. This plan is implemented in the app (src/pages/gym/program.ts, the Gym page tabs Log, Plan, Mobility, Skills, Progress, Body, and The One at /one). This file stays as the written reference for the reasoning behind the program.

## 1. Goals
- Strength for bodyweight: bench, deadlift, pull-ups so heavy that basics feel like warm-ups.
- Skills: clean muscle-up, handstand and handstand push-up, flying push-up, one-arm pull-up, planche and planche push-up.
- Size, in this order: shoulders, arms, chest, then lose fat, then legs.
- Tendons and joints: slow lowering, isometrics, heavy slow calves/tibialis/hamstrings, wrist and grip work.
- Flexibility: every day. Back and hips first, then splits, bridge, shoulders.
- Later: hybrid athlete (a Spartan-style obstacle race). Cardio is optional for now.

Starting point (Nov 2026): 22 y, 67 kg. Bench max 45 kg, deadlift max 80 kg, curl 7.5 kg per hand, 2 pull-ups, 10-15 push-ups, 1.5 min plank. Elbow tingles when pushing near max (settled as push-ups improved). Stiff back and hips, anterior pelvic tilt.

## 2. The week
| Day | Type | Duration | The one exercise (1-10 rule) |

| Tue | Workout 2: Pull | ~50 min | Pull-ups (in session) |
| Wed | Mobility day: hips and back | ~35 min | Split squats or light RDL |
| Thu | Workout 3: Deadlift + legs | ~50 min | RDL (in session) |

Morning flow on workout days: wake 7:00, primer 7:05, gym 7:30, back for work. Gym is 5 minutes from the PG. Weekends at home: dumbbells only.
Alternative if Friday feels heavy after Thursday's deadlift: swap Fri and Sat (Fri free, Sat Workout 4). Keep Friday's pulling light either way.

## 3. Daily primer (10 min, all 7 days, before training)
Joint circles, hip 90/90, hip flexor stretch, cat-cow, thoracic rotations, deep squat hold, 30-60 s bar hang, wrist prep (circles, palm lifts, finger extensions), spinal locomotion 15 s, coiling core.
After every workout: 3 min static stretch of the muscles worked. Evening (optional, 10 min): long holds of 60-90 s for splits/pancake/pigeon while watching something.
Static stretching goes AFTER lifting or in the evening, not before heavy sets.

## 4. The four workouts
"+" means superset (do back to back, then rest). Effort: stop 2-3 reps before failure in weeks 1-4.

### Workout 1: Push (Mon)
1. Flying push-up power reps 3 x 3 (first, while fresh)
2. Planche lean 2 x 15-20 s (light touch)
3. Bench press 4 x 5 (start about 35 kg; 3 s lowering, elbows about 45 degrees)
4. Incline dumbbell press 3 x 8-10 + cable lateral raise 3 x 12-15
5. Overhead press 3 x 6-8 (bar 20 kg or dumbbells 10 kg each)
6. Triceps pushdown 3 x 10-12 + cable fly 3 x 12
7. Dips 3 x 6-8 (this is today's one exercise; skip if the elbow tingles)

### Workout 2: Pull (Tue)
1. Pull-ups 5 x 1-2, then negatives 3 x 3 (5 s down) (today's one exercise)
2. Explosive pull-ups toward the chest 3 x 2
3. Chest-supported row 3 x 8-10 + rear delt fly 3 x 12-15
4. Cable curl 3 x 10-12 (slow, mind-muscle) + hammer curl 2 x 10
5. Skill: one-arm chin-up negative 1 x 2, muscle-up transition drill 2 x 3
6. Grip: dead hang 2 x 30 s, fingertip hang 2 x 10 s

### Workout 3: Deadlift + legs (Thu)
1. Deadlift 4 x 4 (start about 60 kg)
2. Squat 4 x 6 (find a weight with 2-3 reps left; week 1 is for finding it)
3. Romanian deadlift 3 x 8 + leg curl 3 x 10-12 (RDL is today's one exercise)
4. Bulgarian split squat 3 x 8 each leg
5. Standing calf raise + tibialis raise, 3 s lowering, 3 x 12
6. Nordic lowering 2 x 3-4 (from week 3)
7. 5 min hip mobility

### Workout 4: Upper pump + skills (Fri)
1. Handstand practice at the wall 5 x 15-20 s
2. Pike push-up on parallettes 4 x 5-8 (today's one exercise)
3. Planche block (about 12 min): lean 3 x 20 s, pseudo-planche push-up 3 x 5, frog stand attempts
4. Incline cable or dumbbell press 3 x 10-12
5. Lateral raise + rear delt fly 3 x 12-15
6. Cable curl + overhead cable triceps 3 x 12-15
7. Optional 3 min burnout (for the after-feel)
Keep pulling light.

## 5. Mobility days (about 35 min)
- Wed (hips and back): 90/90 transitions, pigeon, hip flexor with glute squeeze, hamstring and split work, cat-cow, thoracic rotations, deep squat holds, 60-90 s bar hang. Then the one exercise: split squats or light RDL, 3 easy sets.
- Sun (shoulders and spine): shoulder openers, wall bridge, skin-the-cat, wrist prep, cross-reach bridge, spinal locomotion, coiling core, gentle frog stand 3 x 20 s. Then the one exercise: rows or easy push-ups.
Hold stretches 60-90 s. Strong pull, not pain. Do not force shoulders or elbows to end range.

## 6. The one-exercise rule (all 7 days)
Source: the 1-10 method. Eight exercises, cycled.
| Exercise | Easy dose on non-workout days (10-20 min, effort 5-6/10) |
|---|---|
| Pull-ups | 5 x 1-2 plus negatives |
| Dips | 4 x 6-8 |
| Push-ups | 5 x 8-10 |
| Rows | 4 x 8-12 |
| Pike push-ups | 4 x 5-8 |
| Squats | 4 x 8-10 (goblet) |
| Split squats | 3 x 8 each leg |
| RDLs | 4 x 8 (dumbbells) |
On workout days it is already inside the session. On Wed/Sat/Sun the app suggests the next one not done recently; you can override. It must stay easy, otherwise it becomes a hidden extra workout.

## 7. Free day (Sat)
Anything: home dumbbell session, planche/handstand practice, a run, a sport, or just the one exercise. Can be moved to any day. Minimum = the one exercise.

## 8. Optional cardio (never required)
- A button on Today. Choices: easy zone-2 run 15-30 min, intervals 4 x 200-400 m with walking, jump rope, incline walk, rower.
- Best on Wed/Sat/Sun or after lifting. Not before heavy lifting.
- Start 1-2 a week; add no more than 10 percent a week.
- It never replaces a workout or the one exercise. Gives bonus XP. Skip when run down.
- Path toward a Spartan-style race: build a 5K easy run, then add carries, hangs, burpees. Not before month 4.

## 9. Skill ladders (each step has a gate)
- Planche: lean (gate 3 x 30 s) -> pseudo-planche push-up (gate 3 x 10 deep lean) -> frog stand (gate 30 s) -> tuck planche (gate 10-15 s) -> advanced tuck (gate 10 s) -> straddle. Planche push-ups later: tuck -> advanced tuck -> straddle -> full. Realistic: tuck hold 6-12 months, tuck planche push-up 1-2 years, full planche multiple years.
- Muscle-up: 8 clean pull-ups -> chest-to-bar -> transition negatives -> banded -> clean.
- One-arm pull-up: weighted pull-ups -> archer -> one-arm negatives.
- Handstand push-up: pike push-up -> wall hold 30 s -> wall handstand push-up.
- Flying push-up: clap push-up -> explosive push-up -> flying.
Elbow and wrist rule: stop a set on wrist pain or elbow tingling. Planche and muscle-up work load the tendons heavily. Increase difficulty by leaning further, not by holding longer. 3 planche touches a week maximum (Fri main, Mon light, Sun frog stand).

Targets (not promises): bench about 55-60 kg, deadlift about 100-105 kg, pull-ups 5-6 clean, pseudo-planche push-ups 3 x 8-10, first tuck planche holds of 3-5 s, wall handstand 30 s.
Rule: add 2.5 kg to a main lift when every set hits the top of the range with 2 reps left. If weights feel too heavy, drop one step. Squat and overhead press are set in week 1 by finding a weight with 2-3 reps left, then +2.5 kg a week (OHP +1.25 kg).
Accessories: double progression, reps first (e.g. 8 -> 12), then add weight.

## 12. Rules for tough days
- Missed a workout: do not double up. Do it in the next free slot, or drop the accessories.
- Back-off rule: last session felt 9/10 or worse, or you slept badly, or you feel tight: same session at about 70% of the weight with 2 fewer sets.
- Express version (20 min): 1 main lift + 1 skill + stretch. Still counts.
- Soreness is fine. Tightness is not: do the post-workout stretch and the mobility days.
- Three or more missed days in a row: restart at the week before and run the back-off rule.

## 13. Food and body
8-10k steps a day, protein first (about 110-130 g), no liquid sugar or empty sugar, mild deficit (about 0.25-0.5 kg a week). Height not given, so no weight target is set.
